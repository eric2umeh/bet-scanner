Bet Scanner — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Bet Scanner (“the app”) helps you scan football fixtures, compare bookmaker odds, log betting tips, and track hit rate. This policy describes what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account (optional): If you sign in, we use Supabase Auth (email). Your user id links tips you log to your account.
  • Tips you log: Match, market, selection, odds, stake, and result — stored in our database so you can review history and hit rate.
  • App access key: If you set an API key in Me, it is stored on your device only (SecureStore / browser storage) to call our API.
  • Technical data: Standard server logs (IP, request time, errors) on our hosting provider for security and debugging.

What we do not do

  • We do not place bets for you or access your SportyBet / Bet9ja accounts.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not read your SMS, contacts, or location (the app does not request those permissions).

Third-party services

  • Hosting: API and web app may run on Render; database on Supabase.
  • Auth: Supabase (email login) — see Supabase privacy.
  • Odds data: Fetched from configured odds providers (e.g. odds-api.io); we send fixture/book requests, not your identity.
  • Telegram (optional): If enabled on the server, digest messages go to a chat you configure — not from the mobile app directly.

Data retention

Tips and account data remain until you delete them (remove tips in the app) or ask us to delete your account. Server logs are rotated by the host.

Your choices

  • Use the app without signing in when the server allows it.
  • Remove logged tips via swipe-to-delete in the Tips tab.
  • Clear the app access key in Me or uninstall the app to remove local storage.

Children

The app is intended for adults where sports betting is legal. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the app owner via the support email listed on the Google Play store listing for Bet Scanner.
