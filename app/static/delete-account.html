Bet Scout — Request account deletion

Last updated: September 2026 · Developer: Bet Scout (com.betscanner.app)

This page explains how to request deletion of your Bet Scout account and associated data stored by the app.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email from the address you used to sign in to Bet Scout.
  2. Email: eric2umeh@gmail.com
  3. Subject line: Bet Scout account deletion request
  4. Include: the email address of your Bet Scout account, and the words “Please delete my account and associated data.”

We aim to complete deletion requests within 30 days of verifying that the request comes from the account owner.

What we delete

  • Account: Your sign-in account (via our auth provider).
  • Tips & slips: Tips you logged (matches, markets, selections, stakes, results) linked to your account.
  • App settings tied to your account: Where stored on our servers under your user id.

What we may keep (short retention)

  • Security / server logs: Routine hosting logs (e.g. IP, request time, errors) may remain for up to about 90 days, then rotate off as the host retains them.
  • Legal holds: We may keep data longer only if required by law or to resolve abuse / security disputes.

Delete some data without deleting your account

You can remove individual tips and multi slips in the app (Tips tab → swipe or Delete) without deleting your whole account. That removes those tip records from our database for your account.
